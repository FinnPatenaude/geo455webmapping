How to use this interactive birding guide: Select each species to view their observation heatmap. Cross-reference your favorite bird species with potential parks and trail/road locations and plan your next birding adventure or find out what birds are in your backyard!

Important Features: Use the search bar to search for your favorite parks and zoom to their location. Use the layer select toggle to choose from Parks, trails, roads and multiple basemaps. Home button returns to default view. Info button accesses the Map's Info page for more details on the driftless and this map's methodology.

Parks, Trails and Roads: Tree markers show where each park or scenic outlook is located. Brown lines show where important bike trails are located, while gray lines show notable scenic byways.

Sighting Density Heat Map: Heat intensity is weighted by total number of sightings occured in an area, so brighter areas indicate places with higher sighting totals.
